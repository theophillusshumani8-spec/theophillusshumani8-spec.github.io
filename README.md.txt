# Theo's Motorcycle & Generator Lab — Website

## Files
- `index.html` — main page
- `styles.css` — styles
- `script.js` — small interactive scripts
- `images/` — place your images here (logo.png, hero.jpg, gallery1.jpg, gallery2.jpg, gallery3.jpg, gallery4.jpg)

## How to customize
1. Replace `images/logo.png` with your logo.
2. Replace `images/hero.jpg` with your hero photo.
3. Replace gallery images with your own photos.
4. Update contact info in `index.html` (YOUR_PHONE_NUMBER, YOUR_EMAIL, YOUR_ADDRESS).
5. Replace Formspree endpoint: `https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORMSPREE_ID` with your form endpoint or backend.

## Deploy
### GitHub Pages
1. Create a repo and push all files.
2. Settings → Pages → Source → main branch → / (root)
3. Wait a minute, then your site will be live.

### Netlify / Vercel
- Drag & drop the folder in the dashboard to publish quickly.

## Notes
- For production contact forms, use Formspree, Netlify Forms, or your own backend.
- Replace Google Maps iframe `src` with your location embed.
