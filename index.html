About Theo's Lab

The story — Theo's Motorcycle & Generator Lab started from a passion for small engines and practical engineering. Founded by Theo, who studied mechanical engineering and gained hands-on experience with motorcycle systems and generator electrics, the workshop focuses on precise diagnostics, honest service and lasting repairs.

Theo’s background — Theo studied mechanical engineering and has experience in engine diagnostics, ignition systems, CDI design, and small engine overhauls. He applies engineering principles to every repair, delivering reliable and durable solutions.

Mission & Values — Quality, honesty, performance, precision. We believe in transparent pricing, using genuine or high-quality parts, and ensuring every customer leaves satisfied and safe on the road or with stable power.
