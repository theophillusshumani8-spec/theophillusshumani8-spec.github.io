About Us

Theo’s Motorcycle and Generator Lab is a professional repair, servicing, and parts supply workshop specializing in high-quality maintenance of motorcycle engines and generator power systems. We are committed to delivering reliable, precise, and affordable engineering solutions that keep your ride smooth and your power stable. At TMG Lab, we diagnose engines using a technical, engineering-based approach. With expertise in small engines, electrical systems, ignition tuning, and performance optimization, we ensure that every motorcycle and generator leaving our workshop is restored to peak condition.
